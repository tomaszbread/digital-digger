IMPORT AUDIO

Save it whole.
Or cut it first.

Drop an audio file into the app or paste a link to download the audio. Then choose whether to add it to your library or open it in the editor first.

You decide what goes into the library. The app checks filenames before saving, so importing a file won’t overwrite one you already have.

Digital Digger is open source under the MIT license and runs on your computer. Your audio stays on your disk and is never uploaded. Check that you have the rights to use any audio you import.
